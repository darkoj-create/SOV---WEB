  - GPS
  - Mreža
  - Kompas
  - Moja baza
- GPS, Network/Signal and Compass continue to open their existing dedicated screens.
- My Base keeps import/export/clear actions, but inside a dedicated screen.
- Battery screen keeps tracking/battery diagnostics and reset action.
